# One fact per line. Shown by catfact, the terminal greeting and the bar.
Cats sleep 12 to 16 hours a day. Respect the grind.
A group of cats is called a clowder.
Cats have 32 muscles in each ear and can turn them 180 degrees.
A cat's purr vibrates at roughly 25 to 150 hertz.
Cats can't taste sweetness. They lack the receptor for it.
The slow blink is a cat's way of saying it trusts you. Blink back.
Cats walk like camels and giraffes: both right feet, then both left feet.
A cat's nose print is unique, like a human fingerprint.
Most cats have 18 toes: five on each front paw, four on each back paw.
Cats have a third eyelid called the nictitating membrane.
Whiskers are about as wide as the cat, so they work as a fit gauge for gaps.
Cats can jump around five to six times their own height.
The loaf position means a cat feels safe enough to tuck its paws away.
Kneading is a leftover kitten habit from nursing.
Cats meow mostly at humans. Adult cats rarely meow at each other.
A cat's collarbone is not attached to other bones, which helps it squeeze through tight spots.
Cats spend a large part of their waking hours grooming.
Cats see well in about one sixth of the light humans need.
The righting reflex lets a falling cat twist to land on its feet.
Adult cats have 30 teeth.
A cat's tail held straight up is a friendly greeting.
Chocolate, onions, garlic, grapes and lilies are toxic to cats.
Many adult cats are lactose intolerant, so skip the saucer of milk.
Cats chatter at birds through the window. Nobody is completely sure why.
If it fits, it sits.
A cat on the keyboard is a feature of the cat, not a bug in the keyboard. catkeys handles it.
Cats sweat through their paw pads.
A cat's hearing reaches about 64 kHz, far above what humans can hear.
Kittens are born with blue eyes. The adult colour comes in later.
Cats bring you "gifts" because you are clearly a terrible hunter.
